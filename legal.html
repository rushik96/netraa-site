Privacy & Disclaimer

This is a starting template. Have a lawyer review it before launch, especially for GDPR (UK/EU visitors), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, and US health-privacy expectations.

Medical disclaimer

Netraa.ai is a medical travel facilitator. We are not a hospital, clinic or healthcare provider, and we do not practise medicine. Nothing on this website is medical advice. Diagnosis and treatment decisions are made only by you and licensed medical professionals. Always talk to a qualified doctor before making any decision about treatment.

Pricing

All prices and savings on this website are indicative estimates based on publicly available ranges and approximate exchange rates. Actual costs depend on your medical condition, the hospital, the surgeon, the length of your stay and any complications. We give you a written quote from the hospital before you travel.

What information we collect

  • Contact details you give us (name, email, phone, country).
  • Health information you choose to share so we can get quotes for you.
  • Basic anonymous analytics about how the website is used.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your request and arrange your care. We share your medical information only with the hospitals and partners you approve. We never sell your data.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data at any time. Email care@netraa.ai.

How we're paid

Netraa earns a fixed package fee from patients and partnership fees from hospitals and hotels. These fees never increase the hospital's standard rate for international patients.

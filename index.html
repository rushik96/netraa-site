Questions

What everyone asks first.

Is it safe to have surgery in India?

We only work with hospitals accredited by JCI (Joint Commission International) or NABH, India's national hospital accreditation board. Before you commit to anything, you see your surgeon's credentials and talk to them on a video call.

Why is it so much cheaper?

Salaries, property, insurance and admin all cost much less in India, and hospitals there see very high patient volumes. The equipment, implants and medicines are the same international brands used elsewhere.

How does the visa work?

India offers an e-Medical visa for patients and an e-Medical Attendant visa for companions. Both are applied for online, and your hospital's invitation letter, which we arrange, supports the application.

Do the doctors and staff speak English?

Yes. English is the working language of Indian hospitals, and every international-patient team speaks it. For Spanish, French, Italian or Arabic, we provide an interpreter.

How does Netraa make money?

From the fixed package fee shown on this page, plus partnership fees from hospitals and hotels. Those partnership fees never increase what you pay: you're always charged the hospital's standard international-patient rate.

What if something goes wrong after I fly home?

Every package includes follow-up video calls with your surgeon. We send your full records to your doctor at home, and complications covered by the hospital's own policy are handled at no extra cost.

Can my health insurance pay for it?

Some international and employer plans cover treatment abroad. Send us your policy details and we'll help you check, and arrange the paperwork your insurer needs.

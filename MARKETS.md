# Netraa.ai: target market analysis

*Researched October 2026. Re-check the figures every quarter, because waiting lists move.*

## The test

A market is worth targeting when **all three** of these are true:

1. **There's a reason to leave:** long public waiting lists, or private care that's very expensive.
2. **People can afford the trip:** a middle class that can pay $5k–$15k for flights, hotel and treatment.
3. **It's easy to get here:** reasonable flights to India and either English or a language we support.

## Tier 1: launch markets (English-speaking)

| Country | Why they'd come | Key figure | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🇬🇧 **UK** | NHS waiting list | 7.3M cases waiting; 105k+ over 52 weeks (June 2026) | [NHS England RTT via BMA](https://www.bma.org.uk/advice-and-support/nhs-delivery-and-workforce/pressures/nhs-backlog-data-analysis) |
| 🇨🇦 **Canada** | Longest waits in the developed world | 28.6-week median wait from GP to treatment; orthopaedics 48.6 weeks (2025) | [Fraser Institute](https://www.fraserinstitute.org/studies/waiting-your-turn-wait-times-for-health-care-in-canada-2025) |
| 🇺🇸 **USA** | Cost, not waiting | About 1 in 4 working-age adults underinsured; 50% of marketplace-plan holders underinsured | [Commonwealth Fund](https://www.healthcarefinancenews.com/news/almost-quarter-americans-underinsured-survey-finds) |
| 🇦🇺 **Australia** | Public waiting lists | Knee replacement: half of public patients wait about 242+ days | [AIHW](https://www.aihw.gov.au/hospitals/topics/elective-surgery/waiting-times-by-patient-characteristics) |
| 🇮🇪 **Ireland** | Some of the worst waits in the EU | 623k waiting for a first outpatient appointment, 105k for inpatient/day-case (Nov 2025) | [NTPF](https://www.ntpf.ie) |
| 🇳🇿 **New Zealand** | Public waiting lists | Only ~65% treated within 4 months (target 95%) | [NZ Government](https://www.dpmc.govt.nz/sites/default/files/2026-06/gt-factsheet-target-2-mar26.pdf) |

**The UK and Canada are the strongest places to start.** Waits are longest there, many people have savings, there are direct flights to India, and many families already have personal ties to India that build trust.

French also covers **Quebec** (Canada's waits) and Spanish covers **Hispanic patients in the US**.

## Not targeted (for now)

Germany, the Netherlands, Switzerland, Austria, Belgium and Sweden have short waits and strong insurance, so patients there have little reason to travel. Revisit later for specific treatments such as IVF, dental and cosmetic surgery, which insurance often doesn't cover.

## What this means for marketing

1. **The message is "skip the waiting list"** (UK, Canada, Australia, Ireland, NZ, Spain, Italy) and **"skip the bill"** (USA, Singapore, Gulf).
2. **Treatments to lead with:** knee and hip replacements, spine surgery, cataracts, cardiac procedures. These have the longest waits and the biggest savings.
3. **Channels:** Google Ads on searches like "knee replacement waiting list UK" and "hip surgery wait Canada"; Facebook groups for people on waiting lists; patient forums; employers in the US that pay for their own staff's healthcare.
4. **Languages:** English first. Spanish, French and Italian are live. Arabic is the next to add for the Gulf (needs right-to-left layout support).
